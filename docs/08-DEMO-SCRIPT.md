# Final Demonstration Script

1. Sign in as the administrator, show role separation and unlock a prepared locked account.
2. Register a fictional patient and show the protected patient dashboard.
3. As reception/patient, select a practitioner slot and create a ten-minute hold.
4. Record a simulated payment status and show the generated invoice reference.
5. As doctor, record blood sugar of 145 mg/dL and show the high-priority non-diagnostic alert.
6. As dietitian, review the change, publish a low-sugar plan and show the patient notification.
7. As patient, complete a meal, add water and show the weight trend.
8. Exchange a non-urgent care message and show a simulated reminder/retry entry.
9. Submit a two-star review and show the automatically created urgent complaint.
10. As operations manager, open the monthly summary and explain visits, satisfaction and payment totals.

Keep the demo under twelve minutes. Use only seeded fictional records. If an external-looking action is shown, explicitly state that payment, email and SMS are simulated.
